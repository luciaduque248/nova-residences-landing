# Design Direction — NOVA Residences

## Brand character
Editorial architectural luxury: restrained typography, warm stone neutrals, large render photography, asymmetrical but calm composition, generous whitespace. Avoid generic glassmorphism, neon gradients, and dashboard aesthetics.

## Initial color tokens
- Background: `#F4F0E8`
- Foreground: `#151412`
- Muted: `#6D675F`
- Accent: `#A27C52`
- Surface: `#FCFAF6`

These are starting tokens, not a locked palette. Refine them during the design phase if contrast, imagery, or brand coherence requires it.

## Distinctiveness rule
Do not reuse section compositions, hero layout, card language, motion choreography, or decorative motifs from the other landing-page portfolio projects merely for speed. Shared engineering patterns are acceptable; visible art direction must remain project-specific.
