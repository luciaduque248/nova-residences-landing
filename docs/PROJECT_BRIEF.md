# Project Brief — NOVA Residences

## Purpose
This is a fictional portfolio case study built to demonstrate professional landing-page strategy, UX/UI, frontend implementation, responsive behavior, conversion thinking, interaction quality, SEO, and performance.

## Sector
Real estate / residential lead generation

## Primary business goal
Generate qualified leads and private-visit bookings from paid campaigns.

## Conversion actions
- Primary: **Agendar visita**
- Secondary: **Descargar brochure**

## Visual direction
Editorial architectural luxury: restrained typography, warm stone neutrals, large render photography, asymmetrical but calm composition, generous whitespace. Avoid generic glassmorphism, neon gradients, and dashboard aesthetics.

## Planned sections
- Hero + project facts
- Concept / architectural narrative
- Apartment typologies
- Amenities
- Immersive gallery
- Location and nearby points
- Financing estimator demo
- Investment rationale
- Lead form
- FAQ
- Final CTA

## Portfolio integrity
- Brand, company, people, statistics, pricing, testimonials, credentials, availability, and results are fictional unless a future source explicitly makes them real.
- Do not invent social proof or claims that could be mistaken for verified facts.
- The final case study should clearly frame the work as a concept/portfolio project.
